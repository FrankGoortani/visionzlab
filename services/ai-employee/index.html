Managed AI Employee for Your Business

We build an AI employee for a defined role in your business and keep it working for a monthly fee.

Who it's for

Businesses that want the result without having to maintain the technology.

What you get

  • An AI employee set up for one defined role, such as routine bookkeeping or intake
  • Regular maintenance: updates, fixes and improvements on a schedule
  • A monthly report on what it did and where a person had to step in
  • People stay in charge of anything that needs judgment or sign-off

Need more? See AI Team →

How it works

  1. Define the roleWe map the tasks, the rules, and where a person must approve.
  2. Build and launchWe build it, run it alongside your team, then hand it the work.
  3. MaintainWe look after it month to month so it keeps doing the job well.

Questions

Is this cheaper than hiring?

For routine, rules-based work it usually is — a setup fee and a predictable monthly fee instead of a salary.

What does maintenance include?

Scheduled care: updates, fixes within business days, and improvements. It is not a 24/7 on-call service.

Who checks its work?

Your team approves anything sensitive. The AI employee prepares; people decide.
