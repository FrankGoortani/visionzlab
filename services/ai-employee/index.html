Service 04 · Build & maintain

Always-On AI Employee for Your Business

We organize how your company runs into a company brain you own, then put an always-on AI employee to work on one defined role and keep it working for a monthly fee.

Book a free discovery call

Who it's for

Owner-led businesses that want the result without having to maintain the technology.

What you get

  • A company brain: your offers, customers, suppliers, who decides what and how you do things, in one place you own, with a source and owner on each entry
  • An always-on AI employee for one defined role, such as a daily operations brief, document intake, inbox triage, follow-ups, a first pass on quotes, or order entry
  • Research skills written for your industry
  • Regular maintenance: updates, fixes and improvements on a schedule
  • A monthly report on what it did and where a person had to step in
  • People stay in charge of anything that needs judgment or sign-off

How it's priced

One monthly plan based on your team size, with setup included. We agree it before work starts.

Your data

  • Only approved business AI plans, whose terms exclude training on your data
  • Built inside accounts you own wherever possible, and you can switch our access off at any time
  • Every step is logged, and a person approves every action that matters

How it works

  1. Define the roleWe map the tasks, the rules, and where a person must approve.
  2. Build the brainWe connect your tools, write down how you work, and set who can see what.
  3. Build and launchWe build it, run it alongside your team, then hand it the work.
  4. MaintainWe look after it month to month so it keeps doing the job well.

Questions

Does it really work around the clock?

Yes. It keeps working when nobody is at a computer, and anything that matters waits for a person to approve it. Our own support is scheduled, within business days.

How is this different from ChatGPT or Copilot?

Those tools only know what someone gives them. We organize what your company knows, keep it current and put it to work on a defined job.

Is an AI employee the same as an AI agent or an AI worker?

Yes. AI employee, AI worker, AI agent and digital worker are different names for the same idea: software that does a defined job rather than just answering questions. Ours does one role in your business, inside the tools you already use, and your team approves the decisions that matter.

Who owns the company brain?

You do. It lives in accounts you control wherever possible, and it stays with you if you change AI provider or stop working with us.

Which AI does it use?

You choose from our approved list of business plans whose terms exclude training on your data.

Is this cheaper than hiring?

It depends on the scope, usage and human review needed. We agree one monthly plan based on your team size, with setup included, so you can compare the total cost with your alternatives.

What does maintenance include?

Scheduled care: updates, fixes within business days, and improvements. It is not a 24/7 on-call service.

Who checks its work?

Your team approves anything sensitive. The AI employee prepares; people decide.
