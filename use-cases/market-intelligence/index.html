Use case

AI Market Intelligence Agent

An AI agent that watches your market and competitors, and turns it into a brief your team reads every week.

Book a free discovery session

Who it's for

Leadership, strategy and product teams that need to know what competitors and the market are doing.

What the agent does

  • Tracks competitor websites, pricing pages, product releases, job postings and news
  • Reads public filings, industry publications and reports you point it at
  • Flags what changed, why it may matter, and what to watch next
  • Delivers a weekly brief, with sources linked for every point

What stays with your people

Your team decides what it means and what to do about it. The agent gathers, summarises and cites; it doesn't set strategy.

Questions

Which sources can it use?

Public sources plus any subscriptions and internal documents you give it access to. Every point in a brief links back to its source.

How is this different from a news alert?

It reads, compares and summarises across sources, and tells you what changed since last week instead of sending every mention.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most teams start with a handful of competitors and one weekly brief.
