Use case · Insurance and mortgage brokerage

Certificate of insurance requests with AI

Venues, landlords and contractors email asking for certificates. The assistant drafts each certificate from the policy record and flags anything the policy record doesn't show. Licensed staff approve every certificate.

Book a free discovery call

How it works

  1. It starts withEmailed certificate requests
  2. The AI agentDrafts the certificate from policy data, flags what the policy doesn't show
  3. Your teamLicensed staff approve every certificate
  4. It ends withCertificates ready to issue

What the agent does, step by step

  • Reading each request Insured, holder, limits and wording asked for
  • Finding the policy By insured name and policy record
  • Comparing with the policy record Limits, additional insureds, special wording
  • Drafting certificates and notes Nothing is issued

What stays with your people

Licensed staff approve every certificate

Anything can be sent back with a reason, for example:

  • Ask the insurer
  • Talk to the client first
  • Holder name is wrong

What you end up with

  • Certificates ready to issue
  • Drafts written in your voice, ready for a person to edit and send
  • Approved records saved in your broker management system
  • A report you can read, print or share

Built around the person who does this work today: Licensed commercial CSR.

In a real setup

  • Requests are read from the certificates inbox; holders are kept on a list per client.
  • Certificates are drafted from the policy record in your broker management system, never from the request.
  • Licensed staff approve every certificate; nothing is issued automatically.
  • Anything the policy record doesn't show goes to a person, never onto a certificate.
