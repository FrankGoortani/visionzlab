Use case · Insurance and mortgage brokerage

P&C renewal list and remarketing with AI

From the book export, the assistant builds the 90-day renewal list, compares each renewal offer with the expiring term and drafts remarketing notes. The broker decides what to remarket and advises the client.

Book a free discovery call

How it works

  1. It starts withA book export and renewal offers
  2. The AI agentBuilds the renewal list and comparison, drafts notes, checks consent
  3. Your teamDecides what to remarket and advises every client
  4. It ends withA renewal calendar and drafts ready for the broker

What the agent does, step by step

  • Building the 90-day list By expiry date
  • Comparing renewals with expiring Premium, deductibles, limits
  • Checking consent Email only where consent is on file
  • Drafting notes and the calendar Nothing is sent

What stays with your people

Decides what to remarket and advises every client

Anything can be sent back with a reason, for example:

  • Not renewing with us
  • I'll handle this myself
  • Wait for the offer

What you end up with

  • A renewal calendar and drafts ready for the broker
  • A schedule of what is due next, with reminders
  • A side-by-side comparison with every difference marked
  • Drafts written in your voice, ready for a person to edit and send
  • A report you can read, print or share

Built around the person who does this work today: Licensed broker.

In a real setup

  • The 90-day list is built from your broker management system every Monday.
  • Consent comes from your consent records; no consent means a call, not an email.
  • The broker decides what to remarket, chooses the markets and advises every client.
  • Renewal letters use your approved templates.
