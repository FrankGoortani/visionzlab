Use case · Accounting and bookkeeping firms

Client document intake with AI for accounting and bookkeeping firms

Clients upload slips, statements, combined PDFs and phone photos to the portal. The assistant splits, reads, names and files each one by client and year, and compares the file with last year's to list what hasn't arrived. Staff approve every filing and every chase.

Book a free discovery call

How it works

  1. It starts withClient uploads: slips, statements, combined PDFs, photos
  2. The AI agentSplits, reads, names and files; lists what's missing
  3. Your teamApproves each filing and every chase message
  4. It ends withA tidy client file and a missing-documents list

What the agent does, step by step

  • Opening each upload Archives, combined PDFs, phone photos
  • Splitting combined PDFs One document per slip or statement
  • Reading slips and statements Client, year and document type
  • Naming and filing Your naming rule and last year's folders
  • Comparing with last year's file What hasn't arrived yet

What stays with your people

Approves each filing and every chase message

Anything can be sent back with a reason, for example:

  • Wrong client
  • Ask the client
  • Duplicate
  • I'll file this one myself

What you end up with

  • A tidy client file and a missing-documents list
  • A side-by-side comparison with every difference marked
  • Drafts written in your voice, ready for a person to edit and send
  • Approved records saved in your practice management system
  • A batch log you can read, print or share

Built around the person who does this work today: Client services coordinator.

In a real setup

  • Uploads are picked up from your client portal and shared drive as they arrive.
  • Names and folders follow your firm's own naming rule and last year's file.
  • Staff approve every filing; anything unclear is held, not guessed.
  • Chase messages are drafts until a staff member sends them.
