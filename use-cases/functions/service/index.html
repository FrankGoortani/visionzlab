AI Use Cases in Customer service

Customer messages arrive through inboxes, portals, chat and the phone, and each one needs the right owner and a sensible reply. An AI agent logs and sorts them, finds the order or account behind each one and drafts a response from your policies. Service staff own every customer conversation and decide each refund, credit or exception.
