Use case

AI Prospect Research and Account Planning Agent

An AI agent that researches target accounts and drafts account plans, so your team spends its time selling.

Book a free discovery session

Who it's for

Business development and sales teams who lose hours to research before every conversation.

What the agent does

  • Builds a profile of each target account from public sources
  • Finds recent events worth starting a conversation about
  • Maps likely decision-makers and the roles that matter
  • Drafts an account plan and first-call questions for your team to edit

What stays with your people

Your team chooses the accounts, checks the research and owns every conversation. The agent prepares; it never contacts anyone on its own.
