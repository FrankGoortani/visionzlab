Use case · Accounting and bookkeeping firms

Year-end and tax-season document chase with AI

Before tax season, the assistant compares each client's file with last year's, builds a missing-documents list per client and stages friendly, then firmer, reminders on a calendar. Staff read and send every reminder; tax questions go to the accountant.

Book a free discovery call

How it works

  1. It starts withYour client list and last year's files
  2. The AI agentLists what's missing per client and drafts staged reminders
  3. Your teamSends every reminder; the accountant answers tax questions
  4. It ends withA chase calendar and a status report

What the agent does, step by step

  • Reading the client list 5 clients, 42 documents expected
  • Comparing with last year's files Item by item, per client
  • Building each missing list What's still to come, and what may not apply
  • Drafting staged reminders Friendly first, then firmer
  • Placing them on a calendar Up to the firm's target date

What stays with your people

Sends every reminder; the accountant answers tax questions

Anything can be sent back with a reason, for example:

  • Client already sent it
  • Don't chase this client
  • Accountant should call
  • I'll handle this one

What you end up with

  • A chase calendar and a status report
  • A side-by-side comparison with every difference marked
  • Drafts written in your voice, ready for a person to edit and send
  • A schedule of what is due next, with reminders
  • A weekly status you can read, print or share

Built around the person who does this work today: Client services lead.

In a real setup

  • The client list and last year's files come from your practice management system.
  • Reminders follow your firm's own wording and schedule, friendly first and firmer later.
  • Staff read and send every reminder; nothing goes out automatically.
  • Tax questions from clients go to their accountant, never answered by the assistant.
