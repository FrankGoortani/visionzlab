Privacy

What this website collects, why, and how to reach us about it.

Request a call

VisionzLab is a brand of VisionZone One Consulting Inc., an Ontario corporation. This notice covers visionzlab.com. Work we do for a client is covered by the agreement for that engagement.

The enquiry form

When you request a call, the form collects the details you type in, plus the page or link you came from, so we know which part of the site was useful. The form is provided by HubSpot, which stores your submission for us and may set its own cookies; see HubSpot's privacy policy. We use these details to reply to you and arrange the call, and we don't sell them.

Analytics

We count page views and button clicks with Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool we host ourselves. It does not use cookies. The free tools on this site record only that they were used, never what you type into them.

Email

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can reply and follow up.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and protect the service. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email hello@visionzlab.com.
